# Artifact Access Policy

This public repository does not include sensitive malware-derived artifacts such as full IDA databases (`.i64`/`.idb`), decrypted plugin archives, or equivalent binary bundles.

## Requesting Access

Access to private artifacts may be considered for vetted security researchers and organizations.

Minimum requirements:

- verifiable identity
- organizational or institutional email
- clear defensive research use case

Anonymous requests are not accepted.

## Usage Expectations

If access is granted, recipients are expected to:

- use artifacts for defensive research only
- avoid redistribution
- avoid offensive operational use

Artifact requests may be declined at discretion.
